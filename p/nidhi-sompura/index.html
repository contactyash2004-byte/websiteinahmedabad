About her
Hello, I'm Nidhi

A 21-year-old director, presenter and storyteller from Ahmedabad, who loves old doors, quiet pols and the people behind them.

She started as an intern at Zee in 2023. Three years and more than 1,000 videos later, she is the Content Director at Yash Sharma Digital: writing, directing and sometimes presenting the stories herself. Away from work, she walks the old city for forgotten stories with @kahani.explorer.
