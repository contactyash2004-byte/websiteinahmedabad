About Nidhi

Behind the camera, and in front of it.

Nidhi is a 21-year-old content director from Ahmedabad. She plans the shoot, directs it, hosts it when needed, and makes sure it reaches the people it was made for.

She started as an intern at Zee in 2023. Three years and more than 1,000 videos later, she leads content at Yash Sharma Digital. Away from work, she walks the old city's pols for stories, and shares them on @kahani.explorer.
