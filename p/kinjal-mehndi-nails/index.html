    Before you book

    Can I get mehndi and nails in the same booking?

    Yes, that's Kinjal's speciality. Use “Build your combo” above and send the message; she'll confirm the time.

    Do you come home?

    Yes, home service is available. Mention your area in the WhatsApp message.

    Is the mehndi safe for sensitive skin?

    Kinjal uses her own homemade mehndi. Clients with sensitive skin say it suited them with no irritation. If you have an allergy, tell her before the booking.

    Can you do mehndi for all the guests?

    Yes. For bridal and family functions she manages time for all the guests. Share the number of people when you book.

    What are your timings?

    Every day, 8 AM to 10 PM. Wedding-season dates fill early, so book in advance.
