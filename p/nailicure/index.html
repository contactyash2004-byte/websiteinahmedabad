Good to know

Questions

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp 91739 29218. You can also use the nail look builder on this page to send your idea first.

Do you do bridal nails?

Yes. Bridal and function nails are a big part of Nailicure's work: stone, crystal, chrome and customised bridal extensions.

What services are available?

Gel polish, nail extensions, nail art, French and pastel sets, chrome and cat-eye, gel removal, pedicure, press-ons and lashes.

Do you teach nail art?

Yes. Vishwa Shah is a nail artist and educator, and "nail art course" is one of the most-mentioned topics in their Google reviews.

How long does an appointment take?

A Google reviewer listed 30–60 minutes for nail art and extensions. Detailed bridal sets can take longer.

What are the timings?

Open every day, 9 AM to 11 PM.
