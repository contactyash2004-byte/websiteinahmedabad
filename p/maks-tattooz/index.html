Keep it sharp

Aftercare
basics

General healing basics. Always follow the exact routine Maks gives you on the day.

Day 1

  • Keep the wrap on for as long as your artist says
  • Wash gently with lukewarm water and a mild soap
  • Pat dry with a clean paper towel

First 2 weeks

  • A thin layer of the balm your artist recommends
  • Don't pick or scratch the flakes
  • Loose clothes over the area

Avoid

  • Swimming pools and long soaks while it heals
  • Direct strong sun on fresh ink
  • Gym friction on the tattooed spot
