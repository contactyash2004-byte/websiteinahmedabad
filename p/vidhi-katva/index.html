FAQ

Good to know.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. Vidhi works with multiple marketing agencies as their on-camera face for client ads. Send the brief and shoot date, and she'll fit into your team's plan.

Can brands hire you directly?

Yes. Restaurants, stores, jewellers, institutes, real estate and service brands book her directly for ads, reels and launches.

Can you shoot outside Ahmedabad?

Yes, for the right project. Share the city, dates and shoot plan on WhatsApp and she'll confirm availability and travel.

What kinds of videos have you done?

Commercial ads, jewellery and showroom launches, product and food promos, real estate films, interviews, fashion and ethnic-wear shoots, bridal and makeup looks, and city features.

Will you read a script on camera?

Yes. Send it in advance and she'll be ready on set. Short hooks and natural delivery work best for reels.

Do you also do voice-overs?

Yes, for promotional and digital content. Ask on WhatsApp for a voice sample with your script.

Do you shoot for makeup artists and bridal studios?

Yes. Bridal, haldi, mehendi and party looks are some of her most-shared work.

How many videos have you done?

More than 1,000 videos across brands, agencies, makeup artists and media pages over 2.5+ years.

How are charges decided?

By the type of shoot, hours and where the video will be used. Share your brief on WhatsApp for a quote.

How do I book?

Message on WhatsApp with the date, location, what you need and whether you're an agency or a brand.
