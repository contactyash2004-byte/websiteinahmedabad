FAQ

Good to know

Which functions do you do makeup for?

Weddings, engagement, kankotri, ghol dhana, Randal Mata, baby shower, vastu pooja, Navratri and other family functions.

Can my mother and family book too?

Yes. Side looks for family members are a big part of Monika's work, including soft mature-skin makeup.

Is a hairstyle included?

Every look in the gallery comes with a hairstyle: buns with gajra, messy buns, curls or waves. Tell us what you'd like when you book.

Are 2027 dates open?

Yes, bookings are open for 2026 and 2027. Message early for wedding-season dates.

What are the charges?

It depends on the function and the number of people. Send your details on WhatsApp for a quick quote.
