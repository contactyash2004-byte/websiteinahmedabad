Good to know.

Is an iPhone good enough for a brand ad?

Yes. Modern iPhones shoot sharp 4K video, and every reel on this page was made for Instagram-first brands. What matters most is planning, light and direction.

How fast do I get the footage?

Within 48 hours on Google Drive. If you use an iPhone or Mac, she can AirDrop clips to you right at the shoot.

Will you help with the script?

Yes. With a newsroom background in reporting and script writing, she can plan hooks and lines for you.

Can you also be the face of the video?

Yes, as an anchor, host or model, depending on what the brand needs.

How do I book?

Send the date, location and what you need on WhatsApp. She'll reply with availability.
