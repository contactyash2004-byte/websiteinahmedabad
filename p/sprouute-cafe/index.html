The bungalow

A house turned café, a little piece of Goa in the city.

That’s how guests describe it on Google. Step past the small waterfall at the entrance and pick your corner: the garden outside, the quiet ground-floor room, or a lantern-lit table for the gang.

Good to know

Questions

What are Sprouute’s timings?

Open every day, 11 AM to 1 AM.

Where exactly is the café?

In a bungalow at Vallabh Row House, Sandesh Press Road, opposite Jivabhai Tower, Bodakdev.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes. Reviewers mention both indoor and outdoor seating, with a small waterfall and high counter chairs at the entrance.

Can we play games at the table?

Yes. Guests say the staff even offered them games to play, and Sprouute runs card and game nights during festivals.

How do I call a waiter?

There’s a button system at every table to call for a waiter, water or the bill, as reviewers point out.

Is there a new menu?

Yes, Sprouute launched a new menu in November. Ask on WhatsApp and they can share it.

Can I book a table for a group or a celebration?

Yes. Use the mood picker above or send a WhatsApp with the date, time and number of people.
