FAQ

Before your pet's first visit

Is boarding in cages?

No. Boarding is cage-free and leash-free, in a home setting, under the owner's supervision.

Which pets do you board?

Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits and guinea pigs. Tell us about your pet and we'll confirm.

Will I get updates while I'm away?

Yes. Pet parents get photos and videos during the stay, so you always know how your pet is doing.

Can you pick up and drop my pet?

Yes, pick-up and drop is available. Share your area on WhatsApp when you book.

What should I bring for boarding?

Your pet's usual food, any medicines, and their vaccination details. Anything familiar, like a blanket or toy, helps them settle in.

Do you groom cats too?

Yes, cat grooming and cat spa are regular services, along with dog grooming and warm-water baths.
