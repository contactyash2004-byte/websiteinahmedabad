For parents

Questions parents ask

Are admissions open?

Yes. Bundle of Joy announced on Instagram that admissions are now open. WhatsApp or call 095580 58044 to book a visit.

Which classes do you have?

Play Way (playgroup), Nursery, Junior KG and Senior KG, plus a daycare. Summer camps and evening activities run too.

Do you have a daycare?

Yes. Parents in Google reviews mention the caring daycare staff and the daily updates with photos of their child's activities.

What are the school timings?

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM and Saturday 9 AM to 2 PM. Closed on Sunday.

How do I know which class my child fits?

Use the age finder on this page for a typical guide, then the school confirms the right class when you visit.

Can parents visit before admission?

Yes, ask for a visit on WhatsApp and the team will give you a time.
