    Before you visit

    Questions

    Is Celestial pure vegetarian?

    Yes. Google reviewers note the menu is all vegetarian, and Jain food comes up in reviews too. Mention Jain when you book.

    What cuisines do you serve?

    Italian, Pan Asian, Western, Mediterranean, Indian chaats, artisan bakery and in-house gelato, all under one roof.

    Do you have a private dining room?

    Yes. The private dining space works for an intimate dinner for 20, a family gathering of 40 or a celebration with 60 guests.

    What are the timings?

    Lunch 12 PM to 3 PM and dinner 7 PM to 11 PM, every day.

    Is there parking?

    Reviewers mention plenty of free parking. There's also a ramp and an elevator, and high chairs for kids on request.

    How do I reserve a table?

    Call or WhatsApp +91 90813 78780, or use the table planner on this page to send your details in one tap.
