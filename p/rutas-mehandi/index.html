Good to know

Are 2027 bridal dates open?

Yes. Ruta has opened bridal bookings for 2027. Popular wedding dates go first, so send your date early.

Can your team do mehandi for all the guests?

Yes. Ruta works with her team, and reviews mention guests and siders being covered too. Tell her the number of people when you book.

Can you draw our faces or a theme?

Yes. Bride-and-groom portraits, Radha-Krishna, royal themes, elephants and peacocks are all part of her work. Non-figurative bridal designs too.

Do you do other functions?

Engagement, baby shower, Karwa Chauth, Arabic and designer mehandi.

What are your timings?

Every day, 7 AM to 11 PM. Call or WhatsApp 88492 93130.
