Questions

What kind of events do you decorate?

Birthdays, baby showers, anniversaries, festivals and pujas, welcome-home and theme parties, plus grand entries, lighting and complete event planning.

What is the starting price?

Their own rate post says decorations start from ₹2,500. The final quote depends on the theme, size and venue, so share your idea on WhatsApp.

Can you copy a reference picture?

Yes. A recent Google review says the decor was exactly according to the reference image they shared.

Do you decorate at home?

Yes. Many of their setups are done in homes and society flats: balloon arches, flower frames and petal welcomes.

How early should I book?

As early as you can, especially around festivals and weekends. Send the date first to check availability.

Where are you based?

GF-31, Bopal Hatt & Homes Complex, Sun City Road, South Bopal. Google lists them as open 24 hours.
