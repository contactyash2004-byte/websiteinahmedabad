Good to know

Questions

Can I get my eyes tested at the store?

Yes. Optic Planet is an eye care & contact lens clinic as well as an eyewear showroom. Google reviewers mention that the eye testing was precise.

Which branches do you have?

Three in Ahmedabad: Vastrapur (Judges Bunglow Road, opp. Gwalia Sweets), Bopal and Prernatirth. This page shows the Vastrapur store.

Do you sell contact lenses and sunglasses?

Yes: spectacles, sunglasses and contact lenses, plus lens care and frame servicing.

Do you repair or service frames?

Yes. Their own words: “From finding the perfect fit to an expert repair, servicing and lens care, the Optic Planet experience continues long after your purchase.”

What price range are the frames?

Reviewers say there are glasses and frames for each price range. Ask on WhatsApp with your budget and they can suggest options.

How long has Optic Planet been around?

Their Instagram says: Ahmedabad’s trusted eyewear showroom since 2000.

What are the timings?

Open every day, 10 AM to 9 PM at Vastrapur.
