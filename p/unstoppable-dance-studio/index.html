FAQ

Before you step in

My child has never danced. Can they join?

Yes. Beginners are welcome, and teachers bring kids along at their own pace until they're ready for the stage.

Is there a batch for women and moms?

Yes. The ladies batch covers Bollywood, hip-hop and contemporary, Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 5–6 PM. Fees: ₹2,500 a month or ₹6,500 a quarter.

Do students get to perform on stage?

Yes. Students perform at big stage shows like Dance Icon, plus competitions and events through the year.

Do you choreograph weddings?

Yes: couple dances, bride & groom entries and sangeet performances for the family, plus corporate events. WhatsApp your date to plan.

Do you teach gymnastics and aerial?

Yes, there are kids gymnastics classes and aerial dance. Ask on WhatsApp for current batch timings.
