    Good to know

    Questions

    What is an art party?

    A celebration built around a creative activity. Guests paint, make candles, decorate trays or name plates, and take home what they made. Krayons’ Instagram says they were the first to bring the art party concept to Ahmedabad.

    Which occasions do you do?

    Kids’ birthdays, baby showers and baby sprinkles, girls’ days out, kitty parties and creative get-togethers. For full event planning there is also @eventsbykrayons.

    Can you come to our venue?

    Reviewers describe Priya bringing a proper team to their party for the activities. Share your venue and guest count on WhatsApp to plan it.

    Do you run regular classes?

    Yes. Regular drawing classes for kids of all ages and skill levels, and canvas painting classes for adults at the studio with flexible days and timings.

    Do I need to know how to paint?

    No. Adults choose their own painting references, and kids’ classes start from picking up a pencil for the first time.

    What else do you make?

    DIY kits and custom creations: painted name plates, rompers, trays, boxes and more.

    How do I book?

    WhatsApp or call 96010 55752, or use the party builder on this page to send your plan first.
