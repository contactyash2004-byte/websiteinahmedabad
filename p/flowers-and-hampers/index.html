    Questions

    Do you deliver on the same day?

    Yes. Flowers and Hampers does same-day delivery across Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, and midnight delivery too, as mentioned on their Instagram.

    What is the starting price?

    Their own Instagram says bouquets and hampers start from just ₹600. The final price depends on flowers, add-ons and size. Share your idea on WhatsApp for a quote.

    Can I customise a hamper?

    Yes. Hampers are made to order: cake, fresh flowers, bubble balloons with a name, marquee initial letters, teddies, chocolates, beauty products and more.

    Do you make baby shower and godh bharai trays?

    Yes. Baby shower trays with bangles, a teddy and fresh flowers are one of their recent specialities.

    Where is the shop?

    Shop No. 4, Sagar Complex, right opposite Vastrapur Lake. Open every day, 10 AM to 11 PM.
