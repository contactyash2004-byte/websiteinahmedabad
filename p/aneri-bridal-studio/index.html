Meet Poonam

13 years, 1,500 brides, one promise

Poonam Padmani started Aneri to give every bride a look she would love for a lifetime. Today her team handles bridal makeup, hairstyling and skin, so the bride and her family can get ready in one place.

The studio at Raspan Arcade, Nikol is soft-lit and calm, with arched mirrors and plenty of space for mothers, sisters and friends.

FAQ

Good to know

Can my family get ready with me?

Yes. Side-bridal looks for mothers, sisters, bhabhis and friends are a big part of what we do.

Where do brides get ready?

At the studio at Raspan Arcade, Nikol. If you'd like the team at your venue, ask on WhatsApp.

Do you do bridal hair too?

Yes: choti with fresh flowers, braids, buns and waves, matched to your look.

Do you teach makeup?

Yes, Aneri runs makeup classes. Ask on WhatsApp for the next batch.

What are the charges?

Packages depend on the functions and the number of people. Send your details on WhatsApp for a quote.
