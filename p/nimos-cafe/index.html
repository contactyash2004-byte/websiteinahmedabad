FAQ

Good to know

Is the menu vegetarian?

Yes, the whole menu is vegetarian.

Can I work from the café?

Yes. Guests mention fast Wi-Fi, a quiet atmosphere and counter seating, great for a few hours of work with coffee.

Do you host birthdays and kitty parties?

Yes. WhatsApp us your date, number of guests and what you have in mind, and the owners will help plan the setup and food.

How late are you open?

Usually until 11:30 PM. During Navratri, Nimo's stayed open till 4 AM for the post-garba crowd. Follow Instagram for special timings.

Is there parking?

Guests mention free parking nearby. The café is in Gamara Capital, behind AUDA Garden, near the SOBO Centre.
