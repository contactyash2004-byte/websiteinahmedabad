    Good to know

    Questions

    What are the timings?

    Open every day, 11:30 AM to 10 PM.

    Where is YooHoo?

    215, Orbit 1, Rajpath Rangoli Road, PRL Colony, Bodakdev. Reviewers suggest using the dedicated parking, as street parking can be tricky.

    Do you host birthday parties?

    Yes. Themed birthdays are a big part of YooHoo: decor, favourite characters, big slides and hands-on activities like the slime cart and custom denim jackets.

    What is there for kids to do?

    Ball pit and big slides, a sand and stone sensory area, princess dress-up, a motor mechanic corner, trampolines, the slime cart and regular art workshops.

    Is there something for parents?

    Yes. There is a café / food court on site, and reviewers mention board games for parents too.

    Which slot should I book?

    One reviewer’s tip: the 2-hour slot is the most economical. Message on WhatsApp for current slots and prices.

    Do you run events and workshops?

    Yes: Halloween parties, a Diwali Arty Party with Bonding 2 Books, slime parties and seasonal workshops. Follow @yoohooplayindia for the next one.
