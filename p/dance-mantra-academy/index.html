Good to know

FAQ

I’m a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes. Mantra Methods runs from beginner to advanced, and Google reviewers call it a great place for beginners and professional dancers alike.

What is Mantra Methods?

Dance Mantra’s structured training programme to help you become a confident, independent dancer. Train 1 to 4 days a week across Groove, Exploration and Performance Lab, plus Footwork Fridays. The new batch starts 30 November.

Do you have classes for kids?

Yes. The Kids’ Dance Training Program runs Mon, Wed and Fri, 7–8 PM, building rhythm, coordination, confidence and real dance skills. Send “KIDS” on WhatsApp to enrol.

Do you choreograph sangeets and weddings?

Yes. Wedding and sangeet choreography for both the bride’s and the groom’s side is a big part of the studio’s work. Book early in wedding season.

Do you do corporate shows and 1:1 training?

Yes, both are listed on Dance Mantra’s Instagram. Message with your dates and group size.

What are the studio timings?

Monday to Saturday 12 PM – 9 PM, Sunday 10 AM – 7 PM.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the programme and how many days a week you train. Message on WhatsApp for current fees and batch timings.
