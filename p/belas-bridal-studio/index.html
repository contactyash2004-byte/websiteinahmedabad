    FAQ

    Before you book a date

    Can I book only a hairstyle?

    Yes. Bridal hairstyle bookings are taken on their own too. Send your function, date and a photo of your outfit on WhatsApp.

    Which functions do you do?

    Mandap / wedding, engagement, reception, sangeet and festive looks, plus sider and family makeovers.

    Can my family get ready with me?

    Yes. Families regularly get ready together at the studio, with sider makeup and hairstyles for mothers, sisters and relatives.

    Do you also run a salon?

    Yes, the studio is a full salon too, including the Protein signature treatment for straight, smooth hair and festive-season services.

    When is the next master class?

    Batches are announced on Instagram with limited seats. Message on WhatsApp to get the next date and fees.
