Before you step in

Questions

I have never trained before. Can I join?

Yes. Students say it’s an ideal place to learn MMA from scratch or sharpen existing skills. Start with a trial session.

What can I learn at Pro Action?

MMA, Muay Thai, boxing, kickboxing, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, grappling and self-defence, with technical sparring.

What are the timings?

Open every day, 6:30 AM to 10 PM. Message on WhatsApp for the batch that fits your schedule.

Who is the coach?

Guru Hemang Prajapati: a UFC-certified coach with 16 years in the game, and an international qualified referee and judge in MMA.

Can it help with weight loss?

Yes. Aayush Palany started at 103 kg and reached 89 kg, a 14 kg loss, training at Pro Action Academy.

Do your students compete?

Yes. Govind Baghel won his first amateur fight at the Warrior Dream Series with Pro Action Academy.

Is there another branch?

Yes, in Pal, Surat. This page is for the Shivranjani, Satellite academy.
