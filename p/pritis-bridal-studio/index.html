Meet the artist

Priti Padia, artist, educator, mentor.

Priti's work runs from Ahmedabad weddings to celebrity sets. The goal is the same every time: bring out natural beauty with a flawless, camera-ready finish.

Her studio in Krishna Baug, Maninagar, also runs a makeup academy and master classes, plus hair, skin and salon services under one roof.

Questions

Good to know

Do you take bookings outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Priti takes bridal and celebrity bookings worldwide. Share your city and dates on WhatsApp.

Can my family get ready with me?

Yes. Family and friends can book makeup and hair for the same function. Tell us how many people when you enquire.

Do you also offer hair and skin services?

Yes. The studio offers hairstyling, haircuts, hydra facials, facials, manicure, pedicure and waxing.

How do I join the academy?

Message us on WhatsApp to ask about the next course or master class batch.

What are the charges?

Bridal packages depend on the look, the number of functions and the venue. Share your details on WhatsApp for a quote.
